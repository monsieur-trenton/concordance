# Sponsors

Thank you to everyone supporting Concordance through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/monsieur-trenton).

## Sponsorship

The GitHub Sponsors page is the source of truth for current availability, tiers, and benefits. Sponsorship helps keep Concordance available while supporting hosting, carefully controlled AI usage, accessibility, privacy, and continued educational development.

Concordance is intended to remain broadly accessible while becoming sustainable enough to support careful long-term stewardship. Sponsorship helps create room for development without making learner attention, student data, or an ever-growing volume of AI calls the business model.

## What continued support makes possible

Concordance has moved from accepted educational-intelligence architecture into implemented Recommendation and foundational Individual Learner Path runtimes. The ILP can now construct governed individual learning opportunities while preserving recommendation integrity, teacher authority, learner agency, and learner-owned pace. A bounded interaction-recording obligation remains before the accepted ILP boundary is fully closed, and the complete learner-facing experience remains downstream work.

Continued support helps turn that foundation into dependable experiences while protecting the principles behind it. Current priorities include completing the ILP boundary, building the Media Center around authentic language and cultural resources, strengthening teacher-facing evidence and guidance workflows, expanding speaking and communicative evidence, supporting proficiency frameworks and future ILR projection work, maintaining privacy and accessibility, and keeping the current platform and responsible AI services operating.

Authentic culture is an important part of that direction. Concordance should make texts, audio, video, visual art, and cultural artifacts accessible across proficiency stages through appropriately designed tasks rather than reserving authentic culture for advanced learners. Future language expansion and living-language stewardship should also involve qualified specialists and community expertise, with provenance and appropriate review.

Privacy remains essential. Individualized learning should not require surveillance, and ordinary learner behavior should not silently become evidence about motivation, ability, or proficiency simply because it can be collected. Sponsorship supports the less visible work required to preserve those boundaries as the platform grows.

The public repository explains the mission, principles, research direction, and appropriate architectural milestones while the protected LAOS reasoning kernel and production implementation remain private. This balance allows Concordance to participate in research and public educational work while preserving intellectual property that may support future licensing, institutional partnerships, and long-term financial sustainability.

Sponsorship does not purchase a guaranteed feature date, predetermined learner outcome, influence over educational conclusions, or privileged access to learner data. It creates room for careful development grounded in communication, evidence, teacher guidance, learner agency, privacy, and cultural connection.
