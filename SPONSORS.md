## Sponsorship

Current support options are available through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/monsieur-trenton) and [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/Concordance_Learn). Each page remains the source of truth for its current tiers and benefits. Sponsorship helps keep Concordance available while supporting hosting, carefully controlled AI usage, accessibility, privacy, and continued educational development.

Concordance is intended to remain broadly accessible while becoming sustainable enough to support careful long-term stewardship. Its purpose is meaningful participation in languages and cultures. AP French, AAPPL, and other preparation contexts are important applications of that larger proficiency-first work, not the boundary of the mission.

Sponsorship helps create room for development without making learner attention, student data, or an ever-growing volume of AI calls the business model.

## What continued support makes possible

Concordance has moved from accepted educational-intelligence architecture into implemented and accepted Recommendation and Individual Learner Path runtimes. The ILP can construct governed individual learning opportunities while preserving recommendation integrity, teacher authority, learner agency, and learner-owned pace, and its append-only interaction boundary is now complete. Downstream Experience Resolution is also implemented and accepted for the currently supported Concordance-native practice path, while the complete learner-facing experience and additional delivery modalities remain ongoing work.

Continued support helps turn that foundation into dependable experiences while protecting the principles behind it. Media Center source implementation now extends beyond retained media-revision identity and bounded database authority into rights-policy, eligibility, availability, deliverability, and resolution-authority foundations. Source presence does not establish completed qualification, deployment, or production activation. No external provider is admitted through the supported path, and public authentic-media resolution and preview remain closed. Current priorities include qualifying these foundations and completing downstream selection, launch, delivery, and learner-scaffolding work; passing a cleanup and integration gate before migrating the total product redesign; delivering Linguistic Specialist onboarding, content and vocabulary authoring, and feature-proposal workflows after that migration; building the full Operations Health Center after the Specialist release; piloting learner refinements through the same [delivery sequence and acceptance criteria](ROADMAP.md#approved-delivery-sequence); expanding authentic language-and-culture experiences beyond assessment preparation; strengthening teacher-facing evidence and guidance workflows; expanding speaking and communicative evidence; supporting proficiency frameworks and future ILR projection work; maintaining privacy and accessibility; and keeping the current platform and responsible AI services operating.

An isolated learner-facing redesign and an independent new-learner ILP are already being developed alongside Media Center work. Their construction can continue within their approved scope, with cleanup and integration before production cutover; this does not establish a production release. Production migration must preserve educational authority, evidence behavior, learner state, privacy, and the accepted LAOS boundaries.

Authentic culture is an important part of that direction. Concordance should make texts, audio, video, visual art, and cultural artifacts accessible across proficiency stages through appropriately designed tasks rather than reserving authentic culture for advanced learners. Future language expansion and living-language stewardship should also involve qualified specialists and community expertise, with provenance and appropriate review.

Privacy remains essential. Individualized learning should not require surveillance, and ordinary learner behavior should not silently become evidence about motivation, ability, or proficiency simply because it can be collected. Sponsorship supports the less visible work required to preserve those boundaries as the platform grows.

The public repository explains the mission, principles, research direction, and appropriate architectural milestones while the protected LAOS reasoning kernel and production implementation remain private. This balance allows Concordance to participate in research and public educational work while preserving intellectual property that may support future licensing, institutional partnerships, and long-term financial sustainability.

Sponsorship does not purchase a guaranteed feature date, predetermined learner outcome, influence over educational conclusions, or privileged access to learner data. It creates room for careful development grounded in communication, evidence, teacher guidance, learner agency, privacy, and cultural connection.
