# Sponsors

Thank you to everyone supporting Concordance at the [Ami tier and above](https://github.com/sponsors/monsieur-trenton).

## Tiers

Full tier details and perks live on the [GitHub Sponsors page](https://github.com/sponsors/monsieur-trenton), which is the source of truth. Highlighted here:

### Bienfaiteur (Benefactor) — $100 / month

You're not just backing a project. You're keeping the lights on. At this level, your support directly covers a meaningful share of what it costs to run Concordance every month: hosting, the AI APIs that power content generation and the conversation partner, and the voice tools that make listening practice possible. Without supporters at this tier, those costs come out of a teacher's pocket.

## What continued support makes possible

Concordance has now completed the accepted architectural design needed to connect learner performance with future individualized educational guidance. Continued support helps move that foundation into dependable learner-facing implementation while preserving free or low-cost access for learners and public-school educators.

In practical terms, sponsorship helps support:

- the Recommendation Engine runtime implementation;
- the future Individual Learner Path and learner-choice experience;
- teacher-facing evidence and guidance workflows;
- authentic language and cultural experiences;
- privacy, accessibility, safety, and responsible research infrastructure; and
- the ongoing hosting and AI usage required by the current product.

Sponsorship does not purchase a guaranteed feature date or a predetermined learner outcome. It creates room for careful development grounded in communication, evidence, teacher guidance, and learner agency.
