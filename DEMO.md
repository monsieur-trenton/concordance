# Preview Concordance — 2-Hour Read-Only Demo

Current-reference review: November 1, 2026. Source milestones do not establish production availability.

Thank you for your interest in Concordance. The time-limited, read-only demo provides a selected preview of the current product while the broader learner experience and redesign remain in development. It is not a claim that the full platform or redesigned interface is production-complete.

## What the demo shows

The demo presents a student experience at an intermediate French level. You can explore:

- **Adaptive Practice** — grammar, vocabulary, reading, and communication activities;
- **Conversation Partner** — inspect the available dialogue interface and illustrative feedback;
- **Cultural Scenarios** — interactive contexts from across the Francophone world;
- **Speaking Practice** — inspect available speaking-task instructions and examples;
- **Writing Tools** — inspect available sample writing and feedback; and
- **Focus Areas** — how recurring needs can be made visible for further practice.

The demo is an illustration of the current product experience. It is not a formal proficiency assessment, certification, or complete representation of every account role.

## How to access the demo

### 1. Request a link

Visit [concordancelearn.com/request-demo](https://concordancelearn.com/request-demo) and enter an email address. A demo-access link is requested by email; delivery depends on service availability. First use starts one two-hour session, and the link can be reused while that session remains active.

### 2. Open the link

The link provides access without creating a normal student account. The session starts when the link is opened and expires after the published demo period.

### 3. Explore the experience

The demo uses a shared demonstration account with read-only access. It does not create a new personal learner account or enroll the visitor in a class. Access-request and session metadata are retained separately from personal learning history; this guide does not establish a retention period.

## Read-only boundaries

The demo is designed for exploration rather than real coursework:

- it does not create or modify class rosters;
- it does not create assignments;
- it does not represent a real learner's official proficiency;
- activity and feedback are not retained as a personal learning history; and
- some authenticated student, teacher, and administrator workflows are not included.

## Suggested two-hour exploration

**15 minutes — Practice**

Browse available activity types and examples, noticing how explanations connect an answer to a language concept.

**20 minutes — Conversation Partner**

Inspect the available conversation interface and examples. Live dialogue and new feedback are not promised in this read-only session.

**15 minutes — Cultural Scenarios**

Explore one or two Francophone contexts and notice how vocabulary and communication are connected to place and situation.

**10 minutes — Speaking**

Inspect the available speaking instructions and examples. Recording, submission, and new feedback are not promised in this read-only session.

**20 minutes — Writing and Focus Areas**

Inspect available writing examples and how recurring needs are presented as possible areas for further work. New submissions and feedback are outside this read-only walkthrough.

**Remaining time — Free exploration**

Return to any activity that helped you understand the product's approach.

## What to look for

### Evidence before guidance

The product is built around demonstrated performance rather than completion, points, or streaks. Feedback and focus areas are intended to help learners and teachers understand what the work may show.

### Human educational authority

AI can assist with dialogue, feedback, and draft content. Teachers retain judgment about classroom meaning and instructional priorities. Learners retain choice and pace.

### A foundation still being built

Concordance now has implemented and accepted Recommendation and Individual Learner Path runtimes through their intended backend boundaries. Bounded Experience Resolution is also implemented and accepted for the currently supported Concordance-native practice path, and Media Center source now includes retained identity, rights-policy, and resolution-authority foundations.

These are architectural and backend milestones. They do not mean that every individual opportunity, delivery modality, authentic-media experience, or redesigned learner interface is available in this demo. The current redesign remains an isolated preview, and public authentic-media delivery remains closed. Source implementation is distinct from qualification, deployment, and activation; provider admission and the remaining selection, launch, delivery, and scaffolding gates must be satisfied before public access opens.
