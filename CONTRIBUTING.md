# Contributing & getting involved

Thanks for your interest in Concordance Learn. The production application and protected LAOS evidence-reasoning architecture live in private repositories, so this public repository is not where the core application is built. It remains the public home for the mission, product direction, sponsorship model, documentation, and ways to participate.

French is the first implementation, but Concordance is being built around a broader question: how can educational technology help people become more capable, confident, and independent participants in another language and culture while respecting evidence, learner agency, teacher judgment, privacy, and cultural stewardship?

## Ways to help

- **Open an issue.** Bugs in the live French-first product, accessibility concerns, feature ideas, and questions about pedagogy or the public direction are welcome through [New issue](https://github.com/monsieur-trenton/concordance/issues/new/choose).
- **Try it and give feedback.** Use the app at [concordancelearn.com](https://concordancelearn.com/) and explain what supports or obstructs meaningful learning. Feedback from learners and teachers is especially useful.
- **Share your perspective.** Language educators, serious independent learners, applied-linguistics and learning-science researchers, accessibility specialists, and cultural or language experts can all identify assumptions the project should examine.
- **Use the in-app feature board.** Signed-in users can help inform product priorities. Voting is one source of evidence, not a promise that popularity alone determines what is built next.
- **Share Concordance** with a learner, educator, researcher, language program, or potential collaborator who values communication, culture, evidence, and responsible educational technology.
- **[Sponsor the work](https://github.com/sponsors/monsieur-trenton).** Sponsorship supports infrastructure, speech and language services, accessibility, privacy, cultural and linguistic expertise, and the time required to build the roadmap carefully. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for current priorities.

## Documentation pull requests

Small corrections to the public documentation—typos, broken links, accessibility improvements, and clarity—are welcome as pull requests. For larger changes, open an issue first so scope, evidence, stewardship, and protected implementation boundaries can be discussed before substantial work begins.

## Public and protected work

Concordance can publish research questions, evaluation methods, selected schemas, public documentation, and appropriate shared infrastructure without exposing learner information or the protected LAOS reasoning kernel. Contributions do not create access to private learner data, production systems, community-controlled knowledge, or proprietary implementation details.

## A note on conduct

Participation is covered by the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Be kind and specific. Learners, educators, researchers, and community contributors may bring different forms of expertise, and no single person represents an entire language or culture.
